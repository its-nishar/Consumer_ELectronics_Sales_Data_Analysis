# 📱 Consumer Electronics Customer Purchase Intent Analysis

### Data-Driven Analysis of Customer Behavior, Satisfaction, and Purchase Intent

---

## 📌 About the Project

**Consumer Electronics Customer Purchase Intent Analysis** is an end-to-end **Data Analytics project** that analyzes customer and product data to understand purchasing behavior and identify factors associated with customer purchase intent.

The project focuses on understanding how **customer demographics, product categories, brands, product prices, purchase frequency, and customer satisfaction** relate to purchase intent.

The analysis transforms raw customer data into meaningful patterns, comparisons, statistical findings, and business insights that can support **customer segmentation, product strategy, and data-driven marketing decisions**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objective of this project is to understand customer purchase intent in the consumer electronics domain using data analytics techniques.

- Analyze the overall customer and product profile.
- Study customer age distribution.
- Analyze product category and brand distribution.
- Measure purchase intent across different product categories.
- Compare purchase intent across product brands.
- Study the relationship between customer satisfaction and purchase intent.
- Analyze purchase frequency in relation to purchase intent.
- Examine product price differences across purchase-intent groups.
- Compare purchase intent across different customer age groups.
- Analyze category-brand combinations.
- Identify relationships between important numerical variables.
- Apply statistical testing to validate important relationships.
- Generate data-driven business insights and recommendations.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses a **Consumer Electronics Customer Dataset** containing customer-level and product-level information.

The dataset contains **9,000 records and 9 features**.

### Dataset Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| ProductID | Unique identifier associated with the product |
| ProductCategory | Category of the consumer electronics product |
| ProductBrand | Brand associated with the product |
| ProductPrice | Price of the product |
| CustomerAge | Age of the customer |
| CustomerGender | Customer gender information |
| PurchaseFrequency | Frequency of customer purchases |
| CustomerSatisfaction | Customer satisfaction level |
| PurchaseIntent | Customer purchase intent |

## 📈 Analysis Performed

1. 👥 Customer Demographic Analysis
Customer age distribution is analyzed to understand the demographic profile represented in the dataset.
2. 📱 Product Category Analysis
The distribution of customer records across different consumer electronics categories is analyzed to understand the product landscape.
3. 🏷️ Product Brand Analysis
Brand-level distribution is examined to understand how customer records are represented across different brands.
4. 🎯 Purchase Intent Analysis
The overall distribution of purchase intent is analyzed to understand the proportion of records associated with purchase interest.
5. 📊 Purchase Intent by Product Category
Purchase intent is compared across product categories to identify categories with relatively stronger or weaker customer interest.
6. 🏷️ Purchase Intent by Brand
Purchase intent is compared across product brands to identify differences in customer interest between brands.
7. ⭐ Customer Satisfaction vs Purchase Intent
Customer satisfaction is analyzed in relation to purchase intent to understand whether satisfaction levels are associated with differences in customer purchase interest.
8. 🔄 Purchase Frequency vs Purchase Intent
Purchase frequency is compared across purchase-intent groups to understand the relationship between purchasing behavior and future purchase interest.
9. 💰 Product Price vs Purchase Intent
Product price distributions are compared across purchase-intent groups to examine whether customer interest differs across different price levels.
10. 👥 Age Group vs Purchase Intent
Customers are divided into age groups and purchase intent is compared across these segments to identify differences in customer interest.
11. 🔗 Category and Brand Analysis
Product categories and brands are analyzed together to identify combinations associated with relatively stronger or weaker purchase intent.
12. 📐 Correlation Analysis
Correlation analysis is used to examine relationships between numerical variables such as:
- Product Price
- Customer Age
- Purchase Frequency
- Customer Satisfaction
- Purchase Intent
13. 🧪 Statistical Analysis
Statistical testing is used to evaluate whether observed relationships and differences in customer behavior are statistically meaningful.

A Chi-Square test of independence can be applied to examine the association between categorical variables such as Customer Satisfaction and Purchase Intent.
